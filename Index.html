Materials & Construction Specs

Everything an engineer, PM, or inspector needs to know about what we actually build with.

Two-Tone Laminate Acrylic

  • Construction
    2-ply cap/core laminate
  • Thickness
    1/16" standard
  • Material
    Impact-modified acrylic
  • Engraving process
    CO2 laser, cap layer removed to reveal core color
  • Fire rating
    UL 94 HB

    • The laser cuts through the top ("cap") color layer to expose a contrasting core color underneath — that's what gives the tag its two-tone lettering without paint, ink, or fill that can wear off.

    Mounting & Adhesive

  • Standard backing
    3M VHB viscoelastic tape
  • Coverage
    Full-back on slimline tags
  • Alternate mount
    Pre-drilled screw holes (+$0.25/unit)
  • Valve disks
    Pre-drilled wire-tie / chain hole

    • 3M VHB is a permanent structural bonding tape rated for indoor and outdoor commercial/industrial mounting — no mechanical fasteners required for most panel and conduit applications.
